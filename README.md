**Security Engineering | Cloud Security | SecOps | Incident Response | Vulnerability Management**

IT and cybersecurity professional with 20+ years across infrastructure, DevOps and information security. My focus is increasing resilience: understand the environment, identify the risks that matter to the business, prioritize remediation, and verify that controls work.

## Areas of focus

- **Security engineering and IAM:** least privilege, hardening, identity lifecycle, secure automation and control validation.
- **Cloud and infrastructure:** AWS, Azure, Docker, Terraform and Ansible, with attention to access boundaries and configuration risk.
- **SecOps and response:** SIEM, observability, event correlation, evidence quality and practical response decisions.
- **Vulnerability management:** prioritize risk with business context, assign accountable owners, track remediation and verify closure.
- **Governance and resilience:** security posture, awareness, control ownership, recovery readiness and audit evidence.

Python and shell scripting support my automation work. This public portfolio is being developed to make technical decisions, limitations and verification easier to assess.
